    Rotting Oranges

    Graphs · BFS · Medium

    A grid holds empty cells (0), fresh oranges (1) and rotten oranges (2). Each minute, every fresh orange that touches a rotten one on its top, bottom, left or right becomes rotten. Return the number of minutes until no fresh orange is left, or -1 if some orange can never rot.

    [[2,1,1],
 [1,1,0],
 [0,1,1]]   →   4
